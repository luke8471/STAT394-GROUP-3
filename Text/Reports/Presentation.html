Why did we use those tests?

Henze_Zirkler Test Results:
           Test Statistic p.value     Method      MVN

Due to our Henze Zirkler test for both the un-transformed data (p-value <0.001) and the transformed data (p-value 0.046) we can safety reject the null hypotheses and conclude that the data regardless if it is transformed or not does not follow a multivariate normal distribution.
